Create `app/about/page.tsx`. The route is non-dynamic, no `params`, no `searchParams`. It is an async Server Component marked `'use cache'` so the page prerenders at build time and serves from the CDN.

Story content beats (sourced from ROADMAP.md success criteria 5 and REQUIREMENTS.md BRAND-01):
1. Bulbena (the maker), one paragraph
2. The Malaga workshop, one paragraph
3. The build process, one paragraph
4. The Malaga to Sydney journey, one paragraph

Tone rules (UI-SPEC.md):
- No superlatives ("world-class", "unique", "stunning"). Let the structure speak.
- No passive voice on CTAs. Use "Ask".
- No em dashes anywhere (CLAUDE.md global rule). Use commas, periods, or rephrase.

        <p className="mt-6 text-lg text-[#f2f2ee] leading-relaxed">

        </p>
      </header>

      <section className="mb-12">
        <h2>The maker</h2>
        <p className="mt-4 text-[#f2f2ee] leading-relaxed">

      </section>

      <section className="mb-12">
        <h2>The workshop</h2>

        </p>
      </section>

      <section className="mb-12">
        <h2>The build</h2>
        <p className="mt-4 text-[#f2f2ee] leading-relaxed">

The engine is rebuilt, the discs are matched to the chassis, the side panels and seat are made in-house. Parts that cannot be made well are sourced from established UK suppliers. The bike is finished, tested on the road, then disassembled for transit.
        </p>
      </section>

      <section className="mb-12">
        <h2>The journey to Sydney</h2>
        <p className="mt-4 text-[#f2f2ee] leading-relaxed">

The bike clears the road-worthy and historic registration process in New South Wales, then it is delivered to the buyer in Sydney. The whole crossing takes a few months end to end.
        </p>
        <p className="mt-4 text-[#888880] text-sm">
